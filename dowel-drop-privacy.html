Dowel Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Dowel Drop is a puzzle game made by Sanad El Bachari (“we”). This policy explains what data the game collects and why. Dowel Drop does not ask you to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number inside the game.

What we collect

  • Gameplay data: levels started, levels completed or failed, stars earned, number of sticks pulled and hints used.
  • Device and app data: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow it in the iOS “Allow tracking” prompt. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Your game progress (unlocked levels and stars) is stored only on your device.

Why we collect it

  • To understand how players use the game, find levels that are too hard, and improve Dowel Drop.
  • To measure which of our advertising campaigns bring new players to the game.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) – usage statistics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta App Events / Facebook SDK (Meta Platforms, Inc.) – measurement of advertising campaigns. Meta Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads inside the game.

Children

Dowel Drop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and rights

You can refuse tracking in the iOS prompt, reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings, or delete the app at any time. If you live in the European Union or another region with similar laws, you can ask us to access or delete data related to you by contacting us at the address below.
