Privacy Policy – Goat Save the Queen

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Goat Save the Queen ("the game") is published by Sanadisland ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

Game progress

The game does not ask you to create an account. Your progress (unlocked levels, coins, potions, number of goats saved) and a random player identifier are stored only on your device. We do not receive this data. It may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud or Google), which are managed by Apple or Google.

Game statistics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect gameplay statistics: for example which levels are started, completed or failed, sessions, coins earned or spent, and device information (model, operating system, approximate country, app instance identifier). These statistics are associated with the random player identifier created by the game, never with your name or email. Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before using this data for personalised ads. On iPhone, iOS asks your permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change your choices at any time in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
