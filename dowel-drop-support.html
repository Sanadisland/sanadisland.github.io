Dowel Drop

Pull the sticks, drop the ball into its box, and don’t let the tiny houses fall. 50 puzzle levels in a wooden tower that keeps growing.

Support

Found a bug or stuck on a level? Email us at sanadservicebooking@gmail.com and tell us your level number and device model. We usually reply within 2 business days.

FAQ

How do hints work? You have 1 hint. After you use it, it comes back after 3 finished games. A level solved with a hint gives at most 2 stars.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
